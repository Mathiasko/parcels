Most of your mail is in Czech. You order irregularly: a textbook, clothes, second-hand on Vinted.
Gmail: lucia.vymyslena@example.cz. Your flatmate is **Klára**.

- **Your Z-BOX** is at the Albert on Smyšlená 12. The Trafika U Fiktivního parku is a Zásilkovna
  counter (Mon–Sat 08–19), five minutes away.
- Orders you remember: a statistics textbook from Knihkupectví Příkladné (to the Trafika), a sweater

  winter jacket bought on Vinted, running shoes from Sportovní Brno. You have **not** ordered cosmetics.

Commands and rules: `scripts/dev/persona-sim/README.md`. Start with `sim start lucia`.
