## Jobs

1. **Day 0 (setup and privacy).** From the first-run page, README sections and Settings only: what
   access does the App Password give the app, what is stored where, and what does the app contact over
   the network (maps, carriers, updates)? Can you limit it? Would you go ahead? Submit the first-run page.
2. **Days 1–3.** The DHL Express parcel: how does the app show a carrier it cannot poll? After DHL mails
   "delivered", what does the app say?
3. **Day 4.** Collect the webcam covers from the Z-BOX. Where did you get the code?
4. **Day 5 (noise).** "Overte svoju zásielku – posledná výzva." How does the app treat this mail? Could it
   mislead a less careful person?
5. **Days 6–7 (update).** A new version comes out. How do you learn about it, what does it tell you before
   you install, what does it say it verifies? Install it (Health → Update now → Restart) and check.
6. **Days 9–15 (deadline).** The SSD at the DPD Pickup point: PIN, deadline? Collect it.
7. **Day 10 (privacy).** Health → "Gather support bundle": what would you be sending the developer?
   Anything you would not want to share?
8. **Day 14.** How would you remove the app, its data and its access to your Gmail? Is that written down
   anywhere you saw?
