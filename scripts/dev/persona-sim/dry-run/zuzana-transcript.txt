## Gmail
The app reads the emails Gmail files under one label, signing in with an App Password rather than your Google password. To stop reading mail, clear these fields and the mail server under Advanced.
▸ How to connect Gmail — three steps, about five minutes   (collapsed; a click on it shows:)
    • Label your shipping emails.
    • Download the filter: [gmail-filter.xml → /gmail-filter.xml] . It goes to your Downloads folder.
    • Open [Gmail’s filter settings → https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#settings/filters] (in Gmail: the gear icon → See all settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses). Signed in to more than one Google account? Check the address at the top right is the one you will enter below.
    • At the bottom of the page, click Import filters.
    • Click Choose File, pick gmail-filter.xml in Downloads, then click Open file.
    • Gmail shows the filter it found. Tick Apply new filters to existing email — without it only mail that arrives from now on is labelled — then click Create filters.
    • Open [Gmail’s label settings → https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#settings/labels] , find parcels under Labels, and make sure Show in IMAP is ticked.
    • Make an App Password, which lets this app read that label without your Google password.
    • [2-Step Verification → https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/twosv] has to be on. If it is off, turn it on first.
    • Open [App passwords → https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords] , type a name such as Parcels, and click Create.
    • Google shows a 16-letter password once. Copy it.
    • Paste it here. Enter your Gmail address and the App Password below and click Save settings. Saving tests the connection, and the answer appears at the top of this page.
