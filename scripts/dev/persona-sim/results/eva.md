I'm Eva, 42, a sales rep in Fiktívne Mesto (999 61); I travel a lot (Košice days 3–6, Žilina days 10–12), I read my Mac at hotel evenings and I'm not a tech person.

## 4. Problems

### The app did something wrong (or left a gap)
1. **Major. Day 3–6, notifications.** "Still waiting for you · day N" fires twice a day (08:00 and 15:30) for every ready parcel while I'm away and can't collect. 15 such nags in 4 days. E.g. Day 4: "Still waiting for you · day 2 — Diskont Online ... collect by Sat 7 Nov", twice. They buried the one that mattered ("Pickup deadline approaching", Day 5, 07:00). I'd start muting the app by the third day.
2. **Major. Day 3 10:00 to Day 6, the thermos.** The app knew at Day 3 that the deadline was Sat 7 Nov 23:59 and that I'm back Sat evening after the box is out of reach, but it never said "you'll be away at the deadline", never showed the BalíkoBOX PIN (778102 only in e-mail #9) so I couldn't hand it to a neighbour, and had no "extend/ask for return" action. The thermos went back to the sender. Cost me a parcel.
3. **Major. Day 5, failed delivery.** "Delivery attempt failed — Adresát nezastihnutý" showed no next-attempt date and no way to change it; the mail had it ("nasledujúci pracovný deň", "presmeruj.mojbalik.sk"). From Košice I could have redirected the charger.
4. **Major. Day 3, 5, 7, pickup-point info on cards.** PINs for BalíkoBOX (778102) and GLS (662014) are not on the dashboard, though the Z-BOX code is ("Open the box with 120934"). The GLS and SPS cards don't name the ParcelShop / Trafika (Kozmetika Jasmín and Knihomoľ show no place heading at all). I had to scroll back to e-mails #9, #18, #26. Inconsistent.
5. **Minor. Day 7, opening hours.** The app shows no opening hours, and offered "Today, from 13:00" on a Sunday for a ParcelShop closed Sundays (`/settings`/calendar form for parcel 5). I only know the hours from living here.
6. **Minor. Day 8, courier time window.** Notification says "Out for delivery — Kuriér doručuje"; the "17:00 – 18:00" window only in the DPD mail.
7. **Minor. Day 7–10, thermos card.** "Returning to sender" with "Collect by Sat 7 Nov · deadline passed" stays under "In flight" and counts in "4 in flight, ..."; it looked unfinished until Day 10's "Returned to sender". The label "Pickup point" next to it also reads strangely for a parcel going back.
8. **Minor. Day 11, phishing mail is silent.** See Job 7: the app judged it correctly but only on the Attention page; no notification or number. The dashboard summary line ("1 in flight, ready for pickup.") doesn't mention it either.
9. **Minor. Day 14, 390 px History.** The table shows Parcel and Carrier only; Outcome and Date fall off the edge (`results/eva-shots/day14-history-390.png`).
10. **Minor. Day 8 and 13, delivery lag.** The delivery mail came at 17:22 and 10:32, the app said "Delivered" at 18:00 and 11:00 (hourly poll). Not a problem for me, just noticed.
11. **Minor. Day 3 to 6, menu-bar number.** 3 on Day 5–6 counted parcels I couldn't reach. Technically right ("ready to collect") but looking at it from the hotel it reads like homework I can't do.

### The app did it right but I didn't understand
12. **Minor. Day 0, postcode format.** "Five digits, like 81101": I write 999 61 with a space; it wasn't clear whether a space is OK. (I typed 99961.)
13. **Minor. Day 11, Attention wording.** "Emails that did not yield a parcel" and "Assign it if you trust it": I don't know what "assign" means and nothing says "probably a scam".
14. **Minor. Day 2, the second parcel.** Both "Registered" cards have no expected date; I read "No updates yet" as "broken" for a minute before the 07:00 "Registered" toast.
15. **Minor. Day 5 and 8, mail text.** The DPD mails open with "Dobrý deň Jana Testová" — not my name. Carrier mail, not the app's, but with no app-level check I wondered if it was my parcel.

## 5. Missing features that block my day-to-day use
- **"You'll be away when this expires" warning, tied to my calendar or a manual "away" date range.** Day 3, 10:00 at the hotel, thermos "Collect by Sat 7 Nov"; I'm back Sat evening. The app could have said so right then, while someone could still help.
- **Pickup PIN / code and the exact pickup place on every ready card, with a "share" action.** Day 4, Košice, I wanted to text my neighbour the BalíkoBOX PIN for the thermos; it was only in e-mail #9 (and the GLS PIN for the cream, e-mail #18).
- **Next-attempt date and a "change delivery" link on a failed delivery.** Day 5, 11:00, "Delivery attempt failed — Adresát nezastihnutý": I needed "next attempt Mon 9 Nov" and presmeruj.mojbalik.sk.
- **An "I'm away" mode that mutes the "Still waiting for you" nags and only keeps deadlines/failures.** Day 6, six notifications, all six were nags.
- **Opening hours on pickup cards, and calendar slots that avoid closed hours.** Day 7, Sunday 09:00, the cream's calendar offered "Today, from 13:00" and the shop was closed.

## 6. Other ideas
- Courier time window in the "Out for delivery" notification (Day 8, 07:00; the window was in e-mail #21 only).
- A notification, not only an Attention page entry, when mail looks like a scam — "suspicious Packeta mail, don't click" (Day 11, 09:10).
- Show contents ("termoska", "bežecké rukavice") on cards, since I remember my orders by what's in them, not by the shop (Day 2).
- A daily summary toast at ~20:00 instead of several pings, which suits hotel evenings (Day 3–6).
- Make the History table fit 390 px (Day 14).
- Move "Returning to sender / Returned" parcels out of In flight sooner (Day 7).

## 7. Verdict
- **What I use today:** Gmail search for "balík" and the carrier SMS messages; nothing systematic. I lose parcels like the thermos this way.
- **Is Parcels better?** Yes, in places: one list of everything in flight; the Z-BOX code on the card; the red card for a failed delivery; the filter that kept a fake "Packeta" mail out of my parcels. It's not better where my real problem is: being away when a deadline falls. I got through the lost-thermos week mostly on my own reading of the e-mails.
- **Still use it after this week?** Maybe. If setup were someone else's job, yes; the Gmail/App Password setup is the real barrier for me. And if it learns I'm away.
- **What would make me uninstall it:** more days like days 4–6 (nags I can't act on), or another parcel lost while the app "knew".
- **Score:** 6/10.
- **To a friend:** "It keeps all your parcels in one place and warns you about the fake ones, but it still doesn't know you're out of town."
