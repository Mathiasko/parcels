# Persona jan: Ján Vymyslený, 14-day run

## 1. Who I am

I am Ján, 71, retired, in Dolné Fiktívce. My daughter put Parcels on my Mac and asked me to try it; I read Slovak and would rather not read English.
I open the window once a day after breakfast, and notifications make me jump a little.

## 2. Diary

**Day 0 (Sun 1 Nov, evening).** My daughter is not here. I read the first-run page: "Your postcode … Five digits, like 81101" — that I understood, so I typed 99941 and pressed Continue. Then the Settings page opened and I read "How to connect Gmail — three steps, about five minutes". Step 1 wants me to label mail, import a "filter", tick "Show in IMAP". Step 2 wants an "App Password" and "2-Step Verification". I would have stopped at the first English word I did not know. I did not do any of it. The sim then says setup was finished on its own at 20:30. I do not believe I could have done it alone; I would have phoned my daughter.

**Day 1 (Mon 2 Nov).** Nothing to do. Menu-bar number 0. I did not open the window; the sim has me look at the app only on the days I need it.

**Day 2 (Tue 3 Nov).** The bookshop said the atlas was on its way. I opened the window after breakfast at 09:00: "Nothing in flight." I pressed "Refresh now" (I understood that button) and got "No new mail. Nothing is in flight yet." The shipping e-mail arrived at 10:05 and the app found it later. A notification came at 15:00: "Registered — Knihy Pre Vás / Podanie zásielky". I did not look at the window again that day.

**Day 3 (Wed 4 Nov).** At 07:00, before my breakfast, a notification popped up: "In transit — Knihy Pre Vás / Zásielka na ceste". It startled me. At 09:00 I opened the window: "1 in flight. Nothing needs you." The parcel is there under "Knihy Pre Vás" — the name of the shop, not the book, so for a moment I did not recognise it. The Slovak text under it ("Zásielka na ceste · SPU Bratislava") I understood.

**Day 4 (Thu 5 Nov).** Nothing from the app. A mail from my grandson: the eye drops from the pharmacy will come by courier, he says. I read it, nothing to do yet.

**Day 5 (Fri 6 Nov).** At 09:00 the window showed the book still "In transit". No notice that the postman came. At 11:25 a Slovak mail from Slovenská pošta told me the parcel is stored at the post office on Hlavná 1, to be collected by 13.11.2026, and that I must bring my ID and pay 18,50 € cash. That mail I understood fully. At 13:00 a notification came: "Ready for pickup — Knihy Pre Vás … collect by Fri 13 Nov". At 15:30 another: "Still waiting for you · day 1". Opening the parcel page, I saw "Delivery attempt failed" (in red) at 10:20 — nothing told me that at the time. The page does not say what to bring or what to pay; I had to remember it from the mail.

**Day 6 (Sat 7 Nov).** At 08:00 "Still waiting for you · day 2", at 08:15 a mail from "DPD": my parcel cannot be delivered, pay 1,50 € postage within 24 hours at a link. I did not click it. This is a payment demand from an unknown link; I would not pay it. The app did not create a parcel from it. On the Attention page it was listed as "Emails that did not yield a parcel … Assign one to a parcel, or dismiss it as junk", with "no tracking number found in this message (it names a carrier)". I did not understand that and did not press Assign or Dismiss. Notifications again at 15:30.

**Day 7 (Sun 8 Nov).** Two more "Still waiting" notifications (08:00 and 15:30, "day 3"). I do not know what "day 3" counts from. It is Sunday; the post office is shut, so I waited.

**Day 8 (Mon 9 Nov).** I went to the post office at 10:00 (open Mon–Fri 08–18). In the app: "Ready for pickup … Collect by Fri 13 Nov · 4 days left". I collected the atlas with my ID and paid 18,50 €. Back home I opened the parcel page; the button "Collected it?" → "Mark as collected" was clear, and I pressed it. The page then said "Collected — marked by you", the menu-bar number went from 1 to 0, and the parcel moved to History. Earlier, on day 2, the same button had said "Mark as received", so I had to think which one was the one. A pharmacy order (Lekáreň Zdravie Online, eye drops and vitamin D3) was shipped by DPD and a notification came at 15:00: "Registered".

**Day 9 (Tue 10 Nov).** At 07:25 a DPD mail: the pharmacy parcel will be delivered today between 10:15 and 11:15, the courier Jozef will call, and someone else may take it if I give them a PIN. The greeting reads "Dobrý deň Jana Testová" — not my name, so I wondered for a moment whether it was meant for me. I walk in the woods until about 11:00, so I stayed home. The app at 09:00 said "Out for delivery … Kuriér doručuje · Dolné Fiktívce" and nothing about the time window, which I only had from the mail. At 10:52 a DPD mail said it was delivered; at 11:00 the notification "Delivered — Lekáreň Zdravie Online / Doručené" came. The parcel left "In flight" and went to History.

**Day 10 to Day 13.** No parcel news. Quiet days. On day 10 a gardening newsletter came in, which I ignore. I did not open the window on these days; nothing needed me. (I looked at History only at the end of the run, when I counted the parcels.)

**Day 14 (Sun 15 Nov).** I counted the notifications over the fortnight: 12 in total. Nine were for the book (registered, in transit, ready, six "Still waiting" reminders) and three for the pharmacy. The six reminders were the part I did not like: twice a day for four days, and I could not switch them off before collecting. I would keep the app if my daughter set it up and maybe turned the reminders down; I would ask her to remove it if the English screens stayed the same.

## 3. Jobs

**Job 1 — Day 0 setup (Gmail, App Password, filter; submit the first-run page).** **Failed, for me alone.** I submitted the first-run page (`sim post app:welcome postcode=99941`) and read the Settings page. I could not have done the rest without my daughter. Steps: 3 sim text reads + 1 post. Where I got stuck, with the exact words:
- "Label your shipping emails." / "Download the filter: gmail-filter.xml … It goes to your Downloads folder." — I do not know what a "filter" is.
- "Open Gmail's label settings, find parcels under Labels, and make sure Show in IMAP is ticked." — "IMAP" is a word I have never heard.
- "Make an App Password, which lets this app read that label without your Google password." then "2-Step Verification has to be on. If it is off, turn it on first." — I would have stopped here and called my daughter.
- "Paste it here … click Save settings." — I could do this, but only if I had the password on paper.
Also: the first-run page itself was clear ("Your postcode … Five digits, like 81101").
Note: the sim marked setup as done on its own at 20:30 on day 0; I did not really perform these steps, so this is my judgement from the pages.

**Job 2 — Day 2, find the atlas in the app.** **Partly.** I did not see it at 09:00 on day 2 (the mail came at 10:05). I found it on day 3 at 09:00 as "Knihy Pre Vás … In transit". Steps: 3 (text /, post /refresh, text /). Hesitation: "Nothing in flight. Parcels appear here when a shipping email arrives" — I did not know whether the book was coming or not. I expected the book's title and a line saying "on its way"; I got the shop's name. Screenshot: `results/jan-shots/d3-dashboard-390.png`.

**Job 3 — Day 5, the postman did not find me.** **Partly.** The app did show "Delivery attempt failed" in the timeline and "Ready for pickup" at 13:00, but there was no notification about the failed attempt. What I needed — where, how long, what to bring, what to pay — came from the Slovak mail at 11:25: post office Hlavná 1, collect by 13.11.2026, bring ID, pay 18,50 € cash on delivery. The parcel page shows the address and "7 days left" but not the ID or the amount. Steps: 5. Screenshot: `results/jan-shots/d5-parcel1-1280.png`.

**Job 4 — Day 6, the DPD 1,50 € mail.** **Succeeded in the sense that I did not pay**, but the app did not help me decide. I read the mail, did not click the link, and did not press Assign or Dismiss. Steps: 2 (text /attention, mail 6). Hesitation: "Assign one to a parcel, or dismiss it as junk." and "no tracking number found in this message (it names a carrier)". Attention is in English.

**Job 5 — Days 8–12, collect the book and tell the app.** **Succeeded.** I went on day 8 at 10:00 (post office open). Steps: 6 (sim day --until 10:00, sim text /, sim text /parcel/1, sim collect EB222222013SK, sim post /parcel/1/collected, sim text /history). The button "Mark as collected" and its line "Moves it to History and stops the reminders" were clear. Hesitation: on day 2 the same button said "Mark as received", so I was looking for the wrong word. Also the parcel's name is still "Knihy Pre Vás"; the "Name" field below it did not catch my eye.

**Job 6 — Day 9, the pharmacy parcel.** **Partly.** I understood from the DPD mail that the courier comes between 10:15 and 11:15 and that I must be home; the app shows only "Out for delivery" with no time window. Steps: 3 (mail 8, text /parcel/2, sim day --until 12:00). The result: delivered 10:52 (mail), 11:00 notification.

**Job 7 — Day 14, count the notifications and decide whether to keep the app.** **Succeeded.** 12 notifications in two weeks for two parcels (9 for the book, 3 for the pharmacy). Too many: the six "Still waiting for you · day N" reminders were the problem. I would keep it, with the reminders turned down, if my daughter sets it up.

## 4. Problems

Separating "the app did something wrong" from "the app did it right but I didn't understand".

**The app did it right, but I did not understand it:**
1. **Day 0, Settings, "How to connect Gmail".** "App Password", "2-Step Verification", "filter", "Import filters", "Show in IMAP", "label". *Severity: blocker* — I would stop and call my daughter; I could not do setup alone.
2. **Day 2, 09:00, In flight.** "Nothing in flight. Parcels appear here when a shipping email arrives, or when you add one by its tracking number." I did not know if the book was coming. *Severity: minor.*
3. **Day 3, 07:00 notification.** "In transit — Knihy Pre Vás / Zásielka na ceste". Startled, and in English. *Severity: minor.*
4. **Day 5, 15:30 notification.** "Still waiting for you · day 1". I do not know day 1 of what. *Severity: minor.*
5. **Day 6, Attention page.** "Emails that did not yield a parcel", "Assign one to a parcel…", "Detect from number", "dismiss it as junk". *Severity: minor* — I did not press anything; I simply did not know what the page was for.
6. **Day 9, 07:25 mail.** The DPD greeting said "Dobrý deň Jana Testová", not my name. I wondered if it was for me. This came from the carrier's mail, not the app. *Severity: minor.*
7. **Health page (not visited on any day).** I looked at it once at the end only to check the menu. "Health" means nothing to me for a parcel app. *Severity: minor.*

**The app did something wrong, or did not do what I needed:**
8. **Day 5, 11:25–13:00, "Ready for pickup" page.** The page does not say that I must bring my ID and pay 18,50 € cash on delivery; I had to remember it from the Slovak mail. *Severity: major* — I might have gone to the counter without the money or the right documents, and my time would have been lost.
9. **Day 5, 10:20.** The app recorded "Delivery attempt failed" in the timeline but sent no notification about it; the only alert was the "Ready for pickup" one, two and a half hours later. *Severity: minor.*
10. **Day 6, 08:15, DPD payment mail.** The app did not create a parcel (good), but it also never told me that a mail asking for 1,50 € payment through a link is suspicious, and it left the mail on the Attention page without a clear answer. *Severity: major* — money and trust; I would have asked my daughter before doing anything, but a less careful person could pay.
11. **Day 6 to Day 8, 08:00 and 15:30 notifications.** "Still waiting for you · day 2/3/4", twice a day, until I collected the book. I had no way to silence them before collecting. *Severity: minor* — noticed and shrugged, but it added up to six messages I did not need.
12. **Day 2 vs Day 5, the button for the same act.** "Mark as received" on day 2 and "Mark as collected" on day 5, on the same page layout. *Severity: minor* — the app changed the word with the state, and I had to think about which one was meant.
13. **Day 2–8, the parcel name.** The parcel is shown as "Knihy Pre Vás" (the shop) the whole time; I never saw the book's title on the parcel. *Severity: minor.*
14. **Day 9, 09:00, "Out for delivery".** The app shows no delivery time window, so I did not know whether to stay at home or for how long; the mail said 10:15–11:15. *Severity: major* — it decides whether I miss my walk, and I could easily have been out in the woods when the courier came.

## 5. Missing features that block my day-to-day use

1. **Day 8 at the post office (and Day 5 at 13:00):** I needed to know what to bring and what to pay before going to the counter. The parcel page should show "Bring your ID. Pay 18,50 € cash on delivery." — taken from the carrier's mail.
2. **Day 9 at 09:00 and 07:25:** I needed the delivery time window and a plain "Stay at home between 10:15 and 11:15" on the parcel page, so that I knew whether to skip my woods walk.
3. **Day 0 (evening), setup:** I needed the Gmail setup in Slovak, or a step-by-step that a 71-year-old can follow without my daughter. Without it, I stop at "App Password".

## 6. Other ideas

- **Day 2, the parcel name:** show the book's title, or let me name it on the first screen, so "Knihy Pre Vás" becomes "Atlas húb" — I would have recognised it straight away.
- **Day 6 to 8, reminders:** one reminder a day, or a switch to silence them until I say so. Inspired by "Still waiting for you · day 2" at 08:00 and 15:30.
- **Day 3, 07:00 notification:** do not send notifications before breakfast (08:00), or make them quieter. Inspired by the "In transit" alert that startled me.
- **Day 5, 10:20:** a notification for "Delivery attempt failed", so I know the postman came. Inspired by the red line in the timeline that I only saw at 13:00.
- **Day 6, 08:15 DPD mail:** a warning on the Attention page, in Slovak, for payment requests through links. Inspired by "Vaša zásielka čaká na doplatenie poštovného 1,50 €".
- **Day 5 and Day 9:** Slovak wording for the app's status labels (In transit, Ready for pickup, Out for delivery, Delivered). The carrier's text is Slovak, but the app's own labels are English.

## 7. Verdict

- **What I use today to keep track of parcels:** nothing regular. I read the Slovak post-office mails when they come, and my daughter tells me what my grandson and the pharmacy have sent.
- **Is Parcels better than that, and how?** For knowing when the book is ready and when to collect it, yes: the menu-bar number and "Collect by Fri 13 Nov · 4 days left" made it easy. For what to bring and what to pay, no: the mail did that, and the app did not. For the courier's time window, no.
- **Would I still use it after this week?** Maybe. I would keep it if my daughter sets it up in Slovak-friendly terms and turns the reminders down. I open the window once a day, so the app has to be right when I look, not chase me all day.
- **What would make me uninstall it?** Notifications twice a day for parcels I am not waiting for; another payment request from a link that the app does not explain; having to go through the App Password steps again on my own.
- **Score:** 5 out of 10.
- **One sentence to a friend:** "It tells you when your book is at the post office and when to collect it, but it's all in English, it pesters you twice a day, and it doesn't tell you to bring your ID and pay the cash."
