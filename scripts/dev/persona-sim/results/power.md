## 7. Verdict

- **What I use today:** the Terminal install of this very tool (launchd + CLI + browser bookmark + osascript banners), plus Gmail search whenever I need a PIN, an amount or a delivery window.
- **Is the app better?** For the daily loop, yes: the menu-bar number is something I never had and it was right every time, banners click through to the parcel, the Z-BOX card is excellent, Mark as collected/Undo and Add to Calendar just work, and Health with the activity log is better diagnostics than I ever had. It never crashed or needed a restart in 14 days. But it still sends me to Gmail for exactly the details that matter at the door or the counter (COD amount, GLS PIN, delivery window), it can't close a DHL parcel by itself, and the move-in quietly dropped my history.
