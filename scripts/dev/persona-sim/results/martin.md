# Martin Pedálový — 14 days with Parcels

## 1. Who I am

Martin Pedálový, 45, Bicykle Fiktív s.r.o., Kolesovo — bike parts e-shop and workshop. I open the dashboard at 08:00 like a work queue: what supplier stock comes today, which customer returns wait next door, done.

## 2. Diary

**Day 0 (Sun 1 Nov, 19:00).** Welcome page asks for a postcode only — fine, typed 99971, Continue, landed on Settings. "How to connect Gmail — three steps, about five minutes" is clear enough for me (I've set up Gmail filters before). Hesitations: the field says "Gmail address" with placeholder "you@gmail.com" — my shop mail is martin@bicyklefiktiv.example.sk on Google Workspace; nothing says whether that counts. Opened gmail-filter.xml: a wall of comments and one huge query. Nothing on the setup page tells a business user to add `-from:` for their own shop. ## 4. Problems

### The app did something wrong

1. **My own outgoing orders become my parcels**: *major (close to blocker)*. Day 2 12:00 "Registered — Bicykle Fiktív – e-shop"; day 4 10:00 "Ready for pickup — Bicykle Fiktív – e-shop … Z-BOXe", menu-bar 1, 15:30 "Still waiting for you · day 1". It repeated for orders 1043, 1044 and 1045: 4 manual deletions in 2 weeks, about one per shipped order. The mail is plainly a copy *to a customer* ("Dobrý deň Juraj Kupujúci, vaša objednávka …", subject "Kópia: …"), from my own domain.
2. **Missed tracking numbers in supplier mail**: *major*. Day 4 /attention: "Versandbestätigung / Shipping confirmation CP-77158 … no tracking number found in this message (it names a carrier)". The body says "Parcel 1: 01505666666605 / Parcel 2: 01505666666606". Same on day 10 for CP-77201 ("Parcel 1: 01505666666611"). Without me, the parcel appeared only on delivery morning from DPD's own mail, a day late.
3. **A two-parcel order needs two different forms**: *major (time)*. Day 4: "Assign to a parcel" took one number, then the mail left the queue, so the second parcel had to be typed into Add parcel with the name retyped.
4. **Stale /attention items**: *minor*. The CP-77120 mail (day 4) and the CP-77201 mail (day 10) stayed in "Emails that did not yield a parcel" after the parcels existed and were delivered. I dismissed them by hand.
5. **"Last day" is a closed day**: *major for returns*. Day 10/11: "Collect by Sun 15 Nov", and Add to Calendar pre-selects "Sun 15 Nov — last day, 9:00". The Papiernictvo is Mon–Sat 08–19. The app shows no opening hours at all.
6. **Ready-for-pickup without a place**: *major*. Day 8 13:00 "Ready for pickup — Velo Distribúcia · collect by Mon 16 Nov". The notification, card and parcel page have no ParcelShop name or address and no Apple Maps link, unlike the Packeta ones.
7. **No PIN in the app**: *major*. Day 8: GLS's "PIN kód: 660193" was only in Gmail. The app is useless at the counter for exactly the thing the counter asks for.
8. **No next attempt after a failed delivery**: *major*. Day 6 13:00 "Delivery attempt failed … Adresát nezastihnutý". Nothing about "next working day" or the redirect link, both in DPD's mail.
9. **Cash on delivery not shown**: *major*. Day 4: Reťaze & Spol. "SPS – kuriér, dobierka 312,40 €" is in the supplier mail, and parcel #9's page says nothing. I need cash in the till.
10. **Notification title glitch**: *minor*. "CykloParts GmbH # CP-77120", "CykloParts GmbH # CP-77201": the "#" looks broken. History also shows "CykloParts GmbH # CP-77158" under my own name for the parcel.
11. **Duplicate names on cards**: *minor*. Day 8: two cards called "Velo Distribúcia", told apart only by tracking number. The order number (VD-26-1201 vs VD-26-1209) is in the mail the parcel came from but isn't shown.
12. **History at 390 px cuts off Outcome and Date** ("Deli…"): *minor*. Shot `d14-history-390.png`.
13. **"Still waiting for you" twice a day, including Sunday**: *minor*. Day 7 15:30 and day 8–11 08:00 and 15:30 for Monika's return. Fair for a private person; for a shop, once a day on opening days would do.

### The app was right, but I didn't understand at first

14. **"2026 · 12" heading in History**: *minor*. Day 11. It's year · count, but I read it as December.
15. **"Gmail address" vs a Workspace address**: *minor*. Day 0, Settings, placeholder "you@gmail.com". I wasn't sure my business domain counts.
16. **Technical details**: *minor*. Each parcel page has a "Technical details" section with five identical JSON blobs. Harmless because it's collapsed, but meaningless to me.

### What worked well (earned)

Parcels created from *supplier* dispatch mail before the carrier wrote (Velo Distribúcia, Brzdy Plus, Reťaze, Pneu Velo in Czech). Delivered and collected parcels clear **themselves**: I never once needed "Mark as collected". Ready parcels at the same counter are grouped under one heading. Platform "Nová objednávka" mails and supplier invoices were never labelled.

## 5. Missing features that block my day-to-day use

1. **"Not mine / outgoing" with a sender rule.** Day 4 10:00, the menu-bar showed 1 for my customer's Z-BOX parcel, and it happened 4 times in 14 days. I need "never make parcels from mail sent by bicyklefiktiv.example.sk" (or recognise "Kópia:" copies addressed to a customer), set once.
2. **PIN or pickup code and the place on the card.** Day 8 13:00, saddles at the GLS ParcelShop: I had to dig the PIN 660193 and the address out of Gmail.
3. **Supplier vs return at a glance.** Day 5 and day 9, new returns (Monika, Andrej) mixed with supplier stock in one list. I renamed one by hand and gave up on the second. I need a tag or filter (Return / Supplier), ideally automatic.
4. **Read every labelled number in a supplier mail, including multi-parcel orders.** Day 4, CykloParts CP-77158 with "Parcel 1: … Parcel 2: …", fixed with two forms.
5. **Cash on delivery amount.** Day 4/5, Reťaze R-5530, "dobierka 312,40 €".
6. **Next attempt and redirect link after a failed delivery.** Day 6 13:00, Pneu Velo tyres.
7. **Pickup-point opening hours, with "last day" counted on open days.** Day 11, Monika's return "last day Sun 15 Nov".

## 7. Verdict

- **What I use today:** Gmail search ("odoslaná", supplier name) plus the GLS/DPD sites when something is late. Returns: I wait for Packeta's mail or for the Papiernictvo lady to wave at me.
- **Is Parcels better?** For supplier stock, yes. It turns supplier dispatch mails into tracked parcels, clears them on delivery, and the menu-bar number tells me when to walk next door. For returns, about the same as the Packeta mail. For anything at a counter (PIN, hours, place, COD), worse than the carrier mail, because the important line isn't in the app.
- **Still use it after this week?** Maybe. Only if I can stop my own outgoing orders from showing up. Today every order I ship costs me a delete.
- **What would make me uninstall it:** the first time a "Ready for pickup" for a customer's parcel makes me walk to a counter for nothing, or a week of deleting my own orders every morning.
- **Score: 6/10.** "Good at telling you when the supplier stuff lands, but if you run a shop it thinks your customers' parcels are yours, so expect to delete those every day."
