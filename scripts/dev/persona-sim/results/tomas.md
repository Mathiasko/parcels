# Tomáš — 14 days with Parcels

## 1. Who I am

Tomáš Bezpečný, 34, security engineer in Fiktívna Ves. I install nothing until I know what it reads, where it sends it, where it keeps it and how it updates itself. My parcels matter less to me than whether I would hand this thing my Gmail.

## 2. Diary

**Day 0 (Sun 1 Nov, 19:00).** Installed. The welcome page asks only for a postcode and sends me to Settings, which is fine. Then I read everything: the first-run page, the whole README (install, configure, mail ingest, DHL, maps, notifications, updating, your data, troubleshooting), Settings with the "How to connect Gmail" steps, Health, and the comment at the top of the Gmail filter file. The README's mail-ingest section is the most honest piece of software documentation I have read in a while: read-only IMAP (`EXAMINE`, `BODY.PEEK`), label scope is "a convention this app honours, not a boundary Google enforces", filter has no "never spam" action on purpose, map lookups send "the pickup address only". The maps section even quotes the Content-Security-Policy. I liked that. What I did not like: the app itself (Settings) tells a much softer story than the README ("lets this app read that label without your Google password"), the install step asks me to bypass Gatekeeper for an unsigned app, and the README's updating section describes a `git pull` + `npm ci` process while Health talks about a signed download "checked against the same certificate" as an app the README says is not signed by an Apple developer account. Three different stories about updates. I pressed Check for updates: "Parcels is up to date. Checked Sun 1 Nov, 19:00." Postcode 99981 accepted. I'd go ahead, but with reservations noted under Jobs.

**Day 1 (Mon 2 Nov).** Digest: no notifications, one DHL mail "is on its way", labelled. The dashboard shows "1 in flight. 1 has no status; read its latest message." The parcel page says "Never polled: updates arrive by email, in DHL's own words." Honest, and consistent with the README. The mail says it will be delivered Wednesday 4 Nov; the app shows no date at all, just the subject line. I named it "Security keys (UK)" myself; the app named it by waybill number although the sender "FAKE SECURITY KEYS LTD" is in the first line of the mail. Health: "ok | Poller", "ok | Mail ingest", and the Recent runs table shows an hourly poll since 17:00. Fine.

**Day 2 (Tue 3 Nov).** The customs mail arrived at 08:30: "Clo a DPH na úhradu", 23,10 EUR, "Splatnosť: 03.11.2026", due the same day. No notification. The line simply appeared on the timeline. Meanwhile Packeta's "Registered — Kľúčovňa" produced a notification at 18:00 for a parcel that is merely registered. If I had relied on the menu bar and notifications, I would have missed a same-day customs deadline. The README warned me ("status never comes from prose"), but a new DHL mail is still an event, and the app told me about nothing.

**Day 3 (Wed 4 Nov).** DHL "has been delivered. Signed for by: BEZPECNY." Again no notification; the parcel stays in flight with "Your DHL shipment ... has been delivered" as its text and the dashboard still says "1 has no status". I pressed Mark as received myself. The unlabelled GitHub Dependabot mail stayed out of the label, as it should. Attention page: "Nothing waiting."

**Day 4 (Thu 5 Nov).** 09:00 notification "Ready for pickup — Kľúčovňa, Z-BOX Fiktívna Ves, Jarná 1 (COOP) · collect by Thu 12 Nov" and the menu bar went to 1 at midday. At 15:30 a second one: "Still waiting for you · day 1". Six and a half hours after it arrived. That is nagging, not reminding. The parcel page has "Open the box with 551208" in large digits; the notification itself carried no code, which I prefer for a lock screen. I collected at 22:00 and pressed Mark as collected. No map was shown for the Z-BOX, although the address has a street and a town, and Settings says the map is on. Nothing told me why.

**Day 5 (Fri 6 Nov).** The "Overte svoju zásielku – posledná výzva" mail from bezpecnost@packeta-overenie.example.com. Gmail's filter labelled it (it says "Packeta" and "zásielka"). The app: no notification, no parcel, menu bar stayed 0. It sits on Attention as "Emails that did not yield a parcel", with the full sender address and the note "no tracking number found in this message (it names a carrier)". It does not show the body or the link, and it does not say "this sender is not packeta.sk". A careful person sees the domain and dismisses it. A less careful one sees a "Packeta" mail that the parcel app itself has filed as parcel-related, with an "Assign to a parcel…" form under it, goes to Gmail and pays 0,99 €. The app did nothing wrong; it just lends the mail a little credibility by not calling it out. I dismissed it. Also the Packeta "vyzdvihnutá" mail came in at 22:06 and produced no visible change, which is fine: I had already marked it collected.

**Day 6 (Sat 7 Nov).** Elektro Svet's USB hub became a GLS parcel from the shop's own mail at 10:00, two hours before GLS wrote; nice. At 19:00: "A Parcels update is available. Parcels 0.2.0 is out — open Health and press Update now." Health's Update section: "Checks GitHub for a newer Parcels. Updating downloads it, checks it is signed by the same certificate as this one, and restarts the app." Then three bullets of "What is new", one of which is "Security: an update not signed with the release certificate is refused". So does 0.1.0 refuse an unsigned update or not? The paragraph above says it checks; the changelog says the check is new. Not told: the download URL, size, a hash, which certificate, or a link to the release. The Recent runs row says "an update is available (1 commit(s) behind)", git vocabulary in a Mac app. I pressed Update now: "Parcels 0.2.0 is ready. Press Restart to update". No sentence saying the signature was verified. Restart: a "Restarting Parcels…" page, then Health says "Updated to 0.2.0. Running 0.2.0." but also "restarted 0 times", and Recent activity has no line about the update at all, although the README promised every outcome is written there. Fast and painless; just quieter about verification than the text promised.

**Day 7 (Sun 8 Nov).** GLS courier: "Out for delivery" at 07:00, "Delivered" at 12:00, and the parcel moved itself to History with "Delivered | 8 Nov". That is how a polled carrier should behave, and it is the contrast that makes the DHL silence on day 3 look worse.

**Day 8 (Mon 9 Nov).** The SSD: shop mail at 09:00 created the DPD parcel, "Registered" at 11:00, "In transit" at 21:00. Two notifications for a parcel that is merely moving. I'd turn those off if I could; Settings has no notification choices at all.

**Day 9 (Tue 10 Nov).** 14:00 "Ready for pickup — Počítače Fiktív, DPD Pickup Fiktívna Ves – Kvetinárstvo, Kvetná 7 · collect by Tue 17 Nov". Again "Still waiting for you · day 1" at 15:30, ninety minutes later. The parcel page: address, "Collect by Tue 17 Nov · 7 days left", Apple Maps link, calendar. No PIN. The DPD mail has "PIN: 300417" in plain text, and for the Z-BOX the app had pulled the code out fine. So for DPD I have to go back to Gmail, which is the thing this app is meant to spare me. The mail itself greets "Jana Testová", which is not me; the app does not surface the greeting, so that is a shop problem, not an app one.

**Day 10 (Wed 11 Nov, 10:00).** "Still waiting for you · day 2" at 08:00. Health → Gather support bundle. The page prints the whole bundle as text with a "download it as a file" link and says "send it to whoever is helping you"; nothing is sent by the app, which is exactly what I want. Content: version, Node v24.21.0, "OS: undefined", arch, schema version, port, notifier, "Default postcode: set (value not included)", "Mail account: set (value not included)", "App Password: set (never read by this bundle)", mail server and label name, counts by status and carrier, a run log with timestamps, and an activity log where raw carrier strings are replaced with "[not a status code]". No tracking numbers, no shop names, no address. What I would still think twice about: the label name and mail server, the exact timestamps of every parcel event (a timeline of my deliveries), and that Lockpick-set order appears as "posta 1" with its registration time. Acceptable. "OS: undefined" and "Electron: not reported" look like bugs in the bundle itself. The run log also contains the admission "mail: 1 new, 0 parcel(s) created, 0 pickup code(s) found" at 14:00 on day 9, which is my missing DPD PIN in the app's own words. Collected the SSD at the flower shop with the PIN from the mail and pressed Mark as collected.

**Days 11–13 (Thu 12 – Sat 14 Nov).** The lock-pick set by registered post: three notifications in four hours (In transit, Out for delivery, Delivered) and it filed itself under History. A "Security Weekly" newsletter about auto-updater supply-chain attacks stayed unlabelled; apt. Nothing else. I glanced at the menu bar: 0.

**Day 14 (Sun 15 Nov).** Quiet. I went looking for how to leave. There is no "Uninstall" or "Remove" anywhere I was shown. I pieced it together: Settings says "To stop reading mail, clear these fields and the mail server under Advanced"; the README's "Going back to the Terminal install" says turn off Start at login, Quit Parcels, "move Parcels from Applications to the Trash"; "Install the app" lists the data folders; the mail-ingest section says the App Password "can be revoked instantly at the same page". Nobody says to revoke it as part of leaving, nobody mentions the Gmail filter and label I imported, nobody mentions Calendar events or the Logs folder in one place. Five parcels in History, all correct.

## 3. Jobs

1. **Day 0, setup and privacy — partly.** Steps: 1 form submit plus about 14 pages read (first-run, 9 README sections, Settings, Health, filter file). What I learned, from the text alone: the App Password gives the app the *whole* account, read and send; the README says so plainly ("not scope-limited by Google … a convention this app honours, not a boundary Google enforces"), but the Settings page says only "signing in with an App Password rather than your Google password" and "lets this app read that label without your Google password", which a normal user will read as label-scoped. Stored: `~/Library/Application Support/Parcels/config.json` (0600, holds the password in plain text), `data/` (SQLite, raw `.eml` copies of every labelled mail, map tiles), `~/Library/Logs/Parcels/`. Network: Gmail IMAP; carrier sites with my tracking numbers and postcode; Nominatim and tile.openstreetmap.org with pickup addresses (only during polls, never on page view, and I can turn it off in Settings → Pickup map); GitHub once a day for updates (not switchable off anywhere I saw). No telemetry is claimed and the bundle is manual. What I can limit: the map, yes; the update check, no; what the credential can do, no (Google's limitation, but the app could say so where I type it). Would I go ahead? Yes, because the README is the most candid I have seen, with two reservations: an unsigned, un-notarised app I have to "Open Anyway" past Gatekeeper is holding a credential to my whole Gmail, and the in-app text about that credential is softer than the truth. Screenshot: `tomas-shots/d0-first-run.png`. Where I'd stop: nowhere, but at "Click Open Anyway" I would want a hash on the release page.
2. **Days 1–3, DHL — succeeded, with a gap.** Steps: 3 page reads, 1 name save, 1 Mark as received. The app is explicit everywhere: "Never polled: updates arrive by email, in DHL's own words", Health "by email | DHL | not polled", the dashboard "1 has no status; read its latest message". After "delivered" it did nothing: no notification, stays in flight, no status; I had to Mark as received. The customs mail on day 2 ("Clo a DPH na úhradu", 23,10 EUR, due that day) was also silent. Screenshot: `tomas-shots/d1-dhl-parcel.png`, `d3-dashboard.png`.
3. **Day 4, Z-BOX — succeeded.** Steps: notification → `/parcel/2` → collect → Mark as collected (4). The code "Open the box with 551208" was on the parcel page, taken from the Packeta mail (where it is printed one digit per line); the notification deliberately omitted it. Screenshot: `d4-zbox-ready.png`, `d4-zbox-ready-390.png`.
4. **Day 5, noise — succeeded.** Steps: 2 (read Attention, Dismiss). Treated as "no tracking number found in this message (it names a carrier)", no parcel, no notification, menu bar 0. Could mislead a less careful person: yes, mildly. The sender address is shown, which is the tell, but the app does not say the sender is not a carrier it knows, and it offers "Assign to a parcel…" under a mail whose subject is a classic scam. Screenshot: `d5-attention.png`.

Told before install: version 0.2.0, three "What is new" bullets, and the sentence "checks it is signed by the same certificate as this one". Not told: where the download comes from (beyond "GitHub"), size, hash, which certificate, a link to release notes. After pressing Update now it only said "Parcels 0.2.0 is ready"; no "signature verified". After restart: "Updated to 0.2.0", but "restarted 0 times" and no activity-log line. Screenshot: `d6-health-update.png`.
6. **Days 9–15, SSD at DPD Pickup — partly.** Steps: notification → `/parcel/4` → Gmail (`sim mail 16`) for the PIN → collect → Mark as collected (5). Deadline yes: "Collect by Tue 17 Nov · 7 days left". PIN no: the page has no code line, although the DPD mail has "PIN: 300417" in plain text. Screenshot: `d9-dpd-ready.png`.
7. **Day 10, support bundle — succeeded.** Steps: 1. See the diary for the contents. I would send it. Things I would still want to redact by hand: nothing essential, though the event timestamps and the carrier mix describe my shopping week well enough.

Pieces exist (Quit, Trash the app, the folder paths, "revoked instantly at the same page"), but no page says "to leave: quit, trash the app, delete these two folders, revoke the App Password here, delete the Gmail filter and label". ## 4. Problems

**The app did something wrong (or did nothing when it should have):**

- **Major — DHL mail is silent.** Day 2, 08:30, customs mail "Clo a DPH na úhradu – zásielka DHL Express", "Splatnosť: 03.11.2026": no notification. Day 3, 13:05, "has been delivered": no notification, parcel stays in flight. Meanwhile "Registered — Kľúčovňa" does notify. A new mail on an email-only carrier is the only event that carrier will ever produce; that is exactly when a notification is worth something.
- **Major — DPD PIN not extracted.** Day 9, 14:00, `/parcel/4`: no "Open the box with" line; the mail says "PIN: 300417". The app's own run log admits "0 pickup code(s) found". I had to open Gmail, which is the thing I installed the app to avoid. Packeta's code was extracted fine on day 4.
- **Major — Update verification is claimed, not shown.** Day 6, 22:00, Health: "checks it is signed by the same certificate as this one" before, "Parcels 0.2.0 is ready" after, "Updated to 0.2.0" after restart. At no point does it say the check ran or passed, who signed, or a hash. And the 0.2.0 changelog says "Security: an update not signed with the release certificate is refused", which reads as if 0.1.0, the one doing the installing, did not. The README additionally promised "Every outcome … is written to the activity log"; Recent activity has no update line, and Health says "restarted 0 times" right after a restart.
- **Minor — the "Still waiting for you · day 1" nag.** Day 4, 15:30, six hours after the "Ready for pickup" notice, and day 9, 15:30, ninety minutes after. There are seven days to collect. No way to turn it off in Settings.
- **Minor — support bundle fields "OS: undefined" and "Electron: not reported".** Day 10, 10:00. The bundle is about the environment, and two environment lines are empty.
- **Minor — no map for either pickup point.** Day 4 and day 9, both pages had a street and a town, Settings says "Pickup map: On", and no map appeared, and nothing said why. The bundle says "Geocode cache: 0", so it never tried. (Possibly the sandboxed Mac, but the page should say "map not available" rather than nothing.)
- **Minor — git vocabulary in a Mac app.** Day 6, Health → Recent runs: "an update is available (1 commit(s) behind)". The README's "Updating" section describes `git pull`, `npm ci` and `launchctl`, none of which a Mac-app user has. The install section and Health describe a signed download. Three stories for one feature.

**The app did it right but I had to work it out:**

- **Major, for trust — the in-app credential text understates the access.** Day 0, Settings: "signing in with an App Password rather than your Google password" and "which lets this app read that label without your Google password". The README (which the app never points to from this page) says the opposite: the password "can read and send as the whole account". The sentence belongs next to the field, not in the Terminal section of a README.
- **Minor — the Gatekeeper bypass.** Day 0, README install step 4: "Click Open Anyway". A user who follows that for this app will follow it for the next one. Not the app's fault that it is unsigned, but the README could at least publish a checksum to compare against.
- **Minor — the phishing mail on Attention.** Day 5, "Overte svoju zásielku – posledná výzva" shown with the sender and an "Assign to a parcel…" form. Correct behaviour, but a line like "this sender is not one of the carriers Parcels knows" would do more than the form does.
- **Minor — "Default postcode" prefilled with 81101 before I had typed anything.** Day 0, Settings, read before I submitted the welcome page. It became 99981 after I submitted. Harmless, confusing for a minute.
- **Minor — DHL parcel named by waybill.** Day 1, "JJD000888888888801" as the title although the mail's first line says "from FAKE SECURITY KEYS LTD". The other carriers' parcels got the shop name.

## 5. Missing features that block my day-to-day use

- **Day 2, 08:30:** a notification for any new mail on an email-only carrier ("DHL wrote about Security keys (UK): Clo a DPH na úhradu"). Without it the DHL side of the app is a timeline I have to remember to open, and a same-day customs deadline went by in silence.
- **Day 9, 14:00:** the DPD Pickup PIN on the parcel page, the way the Z-BOX code is. Collect-by-date without the PIN is half the job.
- **Day 0, Settings:** a one-sentence truth next to the App Password field: "this password can read and send as your whole Gmail account; Parcels only ever opens the label read-only; revoke it at … when you stop". The README has the words already.
- **Day 14:** an "Uninstalling" section, in the README and ideally in Settings → Advanced: quit, trash the app, the two Library folders, revoke the App Password, delete the Gmail filter and label.

## 6. Other ideas

- **Day 6, Update section:** show "Signature: verified against <cert name>" and a SHA-256 of the download, and link the GitHub release. (Even "release certificate" named would help.)
- **Day 4 and 9, 15:30:** a Settings choice for which notifications I want: ready-for-pickup only, or everything. Also a choice of the reminder cadence.
- **Day 5, Attention:** flag senders whose domain is not a known carrier or a shop from my own filter, in one line.
- **Day 10, bundle:** add the OS version properly; the field is already there.
- **Day 0:** a "What Parcels sends where" page in the app itself (Gmail, carriers, OpenStreetMap, GitHub), with the switches beside each. It is all in the README; the app has room for it under Health or Settings.
- **Day 1:** take the expected delivery date from DHL's mail ("will be delivered on Wednesday, 4 November 2026") and show it as a date, not only as prose.

## 7. Verdict

- **What I use today:** Gmail search and the carriers' own tracking pages. No carrier apps; I do not give them a phone.
- **Is Parcels better?** For the polled carriers, yes: the Z-BOX code and the collect-by date on one page, a menu-bar number that is 0 when nothing is waiting, and a History that filled itself in. For DHL it is a filing cabinet. The README is the most honest statement of what a piece of software reads, stores and sends that I have read in years; the app's own pages do not quite live up to it yet.
- **Would I still use it after this week?** Maybe. Yes if the App Password text in Settings tells the truth and the update page shows me what it verified. Until then it stays on a machine I do not care about, with a second Gmail account.
- **What would make me uninstall it:** an update that arrives without any visible verification result after the page promised one; the bundle or the log ever carrying a mail address; or discovering a network destination that the README does not list.
- **Score: 6/10.** "The README tells you more about what it does with your mail than Google's own App Password page does; the app itself is quiet where it should be loud, and loud where it should be quiet."
