# Zuzana: 14 days with Parcels

## 1. Who I am

Zuzana, 29, office worker in Fiktívna Bystrica. I order from AliExpress, Temu and Shein constantly and I look at the menu-bar number about ten times a day; I open the window only when the number changes or a notification looks important.
I skim, I hate long text, and my window is often narrow (390 px) beside other windows.

## 2. Diary
**Day 0 (Sun 1 Nov, 19:00).** Opened first-run page: one field, "Your postcode ... Five digits, like 81101". I typed "999 31" (how it's written on my letters, with a space) and it was accepted; Settings later shows "99931". Fine. Then Settings: "How to connect Gmail — three steps, about five minutes" is actually 3 + 6 + 3 lettered sub-steps, with 5 links to google.com pages. On a 390 px window it is a long scroll. I'd have given up at "Import filters ... Choose File ... Apply new filters to existing email" and at "App Password" ("2-Step Verification has to be on") — I don't know what those are; I'd ask a colleague. ## 3. Jobs

### Job 1 — Day 0: set up on my own
**Partly (I'd stop) — failed as me.** About 6 steps: `sim text app:first-run`, README install section, `/settings`, post of first-run (`postcode='999 31'`), screenshots, mails.
- First-run page: fine, one field, one button. I typed "999 31" with a space and it accepted it and Settings showed "99931". (The hint says "Five digits, like 81101".)
- README "Install the app": 5 numbered steps plus 3 sub-steps for the "cannot check Parcels for malicious software" warning. **"Click Open Anyway ... then click Open Anyway once more"**: I'd stop here the first time ("malicious software" = I don't want to touch it). I'd phone a colleague.
- Settings: "How to connect Gmail — three steps, about five minutes" is really 3 steps with 6 + 3 sub-steps and 5 links to google.com. The words I'd give up on: **"Tick Apply new filters to existing email"**, **"Show in IMAP is ticked"**, **"2-Step Verification has to be on"**, **"16-letter password"** (the field then says "16-character"). Realistically 5 minutes becomes 30 and I never reach "Paste it here".
- The page is OK at 390 px, but it's a very long scroll. Screenshots: `results/zuzana-shots/d0-firstrun.png`, `d0-settings-390.png`.

### Job 2 — Day 2 evening: which parcels, where, until when; number match
**Succeeded.** 3 steps (`sim day`, `sim text /`, `sim shot / --width 390`).
- Notifications at 10:00: "Ready for pickup — SHEIN / AliExpress (Cainiao): Z-BOX ... Lesná 4 (Billa) ... collect by Tue 10 Nov" — place and deadline are in the notification. The code is not; I need the window.
- Menu bar: 0 at 08:00, 2 at 13:00 and 21:00, which matched the dashboard ("2 ready for pickup", codes 530912 and 204417, "7 days left"). Screenshot: `d2-dash-390.png`. This was the best moment of the app.

### Job 4 — Days 2-5: noise and scams

## 4. Problems

### The app did something wrong

1. **AliExpress "shipped" mails with a visible tracking number land in Attention as "no tracking number found"** (Day 3 04:20 mail #19 "Tracking number: LP00333333333309"; also #1, #2, #3, #47, #48, #58...). My earbuds, baking mat, LED strip, phone case stayed in Attention for 14 days; the card says "no tracking number found in this message (its subject is about a shipment)" while the text clearly has one. *Major*: it's the app's whole job for AliExpress, and I can't tell what's inside any later "AliExpress (Cainiao)" parcel.
2. **No pickup PIN/place for DPD, GLS and BalíkoBOX parcels** (Day 8, DPD Pickup Station at Kaufland: card has no PIN, mail #53 has "PIN: 448203"; Day 9 BalíkoBOX, PIN 902614 only in mail #61; Day 13 GLS card without a shop name, PIN 573019 in mail #76, shop "Papiernictvo Ceruzka, Školská 9"). Z-BOX parcels show a big code, others show nothing, so I have to go into Gmail anyway. *Major* (cost me time at every non-Z-BOX parcel).
3. **Notification for the GLS parcel has no place**: "Still waiting for you · day 3 — Temu / collect by Thu 19 Nov" (Day 12-14, parcel 17); the card says only "Ready for pickup" under no place heading. *Major* (I couldn't tell where to go).
4. **Cash on delivery isn't shown** (Day 6, Trafika: "You pay 24,90 € (cash on delivery)"); mail #39 had it, app didn't. *Major*: I could have shown up with no card/cash.
5. **The app lags the mail by up to ~3 h** (Day 7 mail 08:09 → notification 11:00; Day 10 mails 07:14/07:19 → 08:00/10:00, I stood at the Z-BOX at 07:40 and could have opened them). *Major*: cost me a second trip.
6. **Collection at the post office isn't noticed** (Day 6 09:30; number stayed 3 and reminders went on, until I pressed Mark as collected). *Minor/major*: the number lies for me.
7. **Menu-bar number counts parcels everywhere** (Day 4 07:40: 4, only 3 at Lesná; Day 12-13: 4, 5). *Minor*, but it makes the number less useful for my route.
8. **Reminders twice a day** (08:00 and 15:30 "Still waiting for you · day N") for each parcel, plus "Delivered" for parcels I just picked up (Day 4 08:00, three at once; Day 14 07:00 four of six). From about Day 11 they are folded into "N parcel updates" with a run-on list in the body ("Still waiting for you · day 1 — Temu; Still waiting for you · day 2 — Temu; ..."). *Minor* (I'd mute it).
9. **Slovenská pošta customs scam gets no warning** while Packeta's does (Day 5 09:30). *Minor/major* for someone less sure than me.
10. **Packeta ad and Temu "free shipping" mail were treated as parcel mail** and clutter Attention (Day 2 #12, Day 12 #84). *Minor*.
11. **Notification titles in English, bodies in Slovak or half and half** ("Registered — SHEIN / Zásielka bola podaná odosielateľom."; some bodies are only "collect by Thu 19 Nov"). *Minor*.
12. **Preview of SPS mail #78 is CSS** ("body { font-family: Tahoma, Verdana, ..."). That's in Gmail, not the app, but it's what I'd see. *Minor*, noted only.
13. **Collected time stamped when I click** ("Sat 7 Nov, 22:00") rather than when I was there; the History table excludes my own clicks from the median. *Minor*.

### The app did it right but I didn't understand

- **"How to connect Gmail — three steps, about five minutes"** vs 9 sub-steps and 5 outside links (Day 0). *Blocker for me.*
- **"Registered", "In flight", "Attention"**: I read "Attention" as "something is wrong with my parcels"; it's actually "mails I couldn't use". I never found out from the nav bar that 4-8 mails were sitting there (no badge). *Minor.*
- **"Dismiss ... deletes the stored copy of the email; the mail in your mailbox is untouched"** and **"Assign to a parcel…"** with a form (tracking number, carrier, name, postcode). I skipped it; too much for a ten-second look. *Minor.*
- The pickup deadline on the dashboard ("Collect by Wed 11 Nov · in 2 days") was clear, but I only saw it when I opened the window. *Minor.*

## 5. Missing features that block my day-to-day use

1. **Day 8, 14:16, Kaufland DPD box, and Day 9, Lidl BalíkoBOX, and Day 13, GLS ParcelShop: I needed the PIN, place and opening hours on the card** (and the same in the notification), as with Z-BOX codes. I went into Gmail every time.
2. **Day 6, 10:30, Trafika: I needed to know I'd pay 24,90 €** (and that it was cash on delivery) before leaving, on the dashboard card.
3. **Day 3 04:20 onwards, the AliExpress shipped mails (earbuds, LED strip, baking mat): I needed those to become parcels named by the items** ("Bluetooth earbuds", "LED strip 5 m") so that "AliExpress (Cainiao)" doesn't appear five times without telling me which is which; on Day 14 the LED strip was nowhere.
4. **Day 4, 07:40, at the Z-BOX: I needed the number to say "3 here, 1 elsewhere"** or let me see just the parcels at the place I'm passing.
5. **Day 6, 09:30 and Day 10, 07:40: I needed the app to be quicker than mail** (the parcel was ready at 10:00 when I came at 09:30; two parcels had been in the Z-BOX since 07:14 when the app told me at 08:00 and 10:00). A "Refresh now" button exists, but I would never press it.
6. **Day 6, 22:00, after the post office: I needed "Mark as collected" from the notification** or a way not to have the app nag about a parcel I'd already collected (no pickup mail from the post office).

## 6. Other ideas

- One daily summary at 07:30 instead of 08:00 and 15:30 reminders (Days 3-14: 4-9 notifications a day for the same parcels). Mute reminders for parcels at a place I'm "not passing today".
- Show where I pass: pin my home Z-BOX as "home" and sort it first (Day 4 and Day 8).
- Notification when I'm 1 day from the deadline only, not every day (Day 8, AliExpress at OC Centrum, 6 days of "Still waiting").
- A "this looks like a scam" label on mails like #17 and #32 (Day 2, Day 5).
- A badge on Attention with the number of waiting mails (Day 14: 8 mails I'd never have noticed).
- Don't send "Delivered" for parcels I picked up in the last hour (Day 4, 08:00).
- Make the notification body Slovak or English consistently (Day 1: "Registered — Temu / Zásielka bola podaná odosielateľom.").
- First-run: offer to skip the Gmail setup and add one parcel by hand to see how it works (Day 0).

## 7. Verdict

- **What I use today:** carrier apps (Packeta, DPD) and a Gmail search for "balík", plus my memory of Z-BOX codes in my phone's notes. Sometimes nothing.
- **Is Parcels better?** For Z-BOX parcels, yes: one list with codes and deadlines, in one glance; and the menu-bar number was right on Day 2, 3, 8. For everything else (post office, DPD, GLS, BalíkoBOX, AliExpress) it's the same as Gmail, because the info that matters (PIN, place, price, what's inside) is still in the mail.
- **Would I still use it after this week?** **Maybe.** I'd keep it if I never had to set up Gmail myself (I could not, see Day 0), and if non-Z-BOX parcels showed their PIN and place. The lag behind Gmail costs me a trip about once a week.
- **What would make me uninstall it:** having to repeat the Gmail setup, missing a parcel because the app was behind or wrong, or the notification spam (4-9 a day) - I'd mute it and then it's useless.
- **Score: 6/10.**
- **To a friend:** "It's a good number in the menu bar for Z-BOX parcels, but for AliExpress and the post office you still dig through Gmail."
